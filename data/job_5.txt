SSC CHSL 2026 – Job Description

Organization: Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
Exam: Combined Higher Secondary Level (CHSL) Examination 2026
Job Type: Central Government Job
Job Level: National Level
Tentative Vacancies: 2,536

Posts Offered
Lower Divisional Clerk (LDC)
Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)
Data Entry Operator (DEO)
Data Entry Operator, Grade 'A'
Educational Qualification
Candidate must have passed Class 12th / Higher Secondary from a recognized Board.
For certain DEO posts, candidates must have passed 12th in Science stream with Mathematics as a subject.
Age Limit
18 to 27 years
Age relaxation is applicable as per government rules for reserved categories.
Job Responsibilities

LDC / JSA:

Maintaining government records and files
Data entry and documentation
Preparing and managing official correspondence
Handling office-related administrative work
Maintaining records and databases
Supporting day-to-day departmental activities

DEO:

Entering and updating data in computer systems
Maintaining digital records and databases
Preparing reports and documents
Checking and processing data
Performing computer-based administrative tasks
Ensuring accuracy of entered data
